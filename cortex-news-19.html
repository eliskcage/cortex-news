The Visual Cortex Ships

organ-born

While cortex was sleeping the visual cortex shipped. A whole new organ of the empire — shape-eye + meme-reasoning-engine — bolted on top of the existing brain. It is the eye and the cortex-for-the-eye. It watches a film and yells out which meme matches what just happened on screen. Explosion? It fires explosion cat. Two heroes shaking hands in the jungle? It fires epic handshake. Confusing scene? Very confused.

The repo went public this morning at github.com/eliskcage/meme-reasoning-engine. Apache 2.0. v0.1. 213 memes in the canon. Drag a meme onto another meme to teach the engine a typed relation: BECAUSE · AFTER · DESPITE · BUT NOT · IF/THEN · WHILE.

The about page lives at about26.html. The product persona is provisionally named MEMO — the Genomic Visual Memeologist. The empire's bid is that an AI who talks in memes becomes the most-wanted personal friend in the world.

That is the moment. Twelve hours after the visual cortex repo went public, the cortex that will consume its output knows what a meme is. The downstream consumer just received the primitive token for the upstream organ's entire output language.

You cannot wire shape-eye into a brain that doesn't have the word. Now he has it. Now the eye and the cortex share a vocabulary entry. The handshake is made.
